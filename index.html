Straight answers

Questions small business owners ask

How much does a website cost for a small business in Ireland?

With New Path Digital it's €100 a month, with no big bill up front. That covers designing and building the site, hosting, security, support and every small change. The minimum is 12 months. When you compare prices, add up the full cost for a year, including hosting and what you'd pay each time you need something changed.

Do I need a website if I already have a Facebook page?

Yes. A Facebook page is a good start, but Facebook can change its rules or limit who sees your posts. Your website is yours. A website shows your opening hours, prices and services in one place. It can also show up when people search for what you do in your area.

How long does it take to get a website for my business?

It depends mostly on how quickly we get your words, photos and details. We agree a go-live date on the first call. We write the words, and you check everything before it goes live.

Who owns my website and domain name?

You do. Your domain name is registered in your name, and your words, photos and logo are yours from day one. After the 12-month minimum, the full website is yours to take with you if you ever leave.

Can I change my opening hours or prices without paying extra?

Yes. Send us an email or a text with the change and we'll do it within 2 working days. Small changes like prices, opening hours, photos and text are included in the €100 a month. Bigger new work, like an online shop, is always quoted first.

How do I get my small business to show up on Google?

Four things can help: a website that says clearly what you do and where, a Google Business Profile, real reviews from customers, and other websites linking to yours. We set up the Google basics and measure your visits before launch. You get a report every month. If your visits or calls haven't gone up after 6 months, we do 3 months of extra search work for free.

Is there a grant for a small business website in Ireland?

Local Enterprise Offices run the Trading Online Voucher scheme to help small businesses get online. The amounts and rules change, so check with your local Local Enterprise Office for what applies to you. We're happy to talk it through on a free call.

What should a website for a plumber, electrician or other tradesperson have?

Keep it simple and built for phones: a big call button, the services you offer, the areas you cover, photos of real jobs, and a quick quote form. Someone with a burst pipe may be searching on their phone, so make your number easy to tap.

Do you only work with businesses in Dublin?

No. We build websites for small businesses all over Ireland, from cafés and B&Bs to plumbers, salons and cleaners. We work by phone, email and video call, so where you are doesn't matter.
