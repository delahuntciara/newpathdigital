One of our agents

The news agent finds possible clients

What it reads

Eleven Irish news feeds, from RTÉ Business to the Clare Echo, plus our description of our customer.

What it makes

A list of leads: the company, why now, the article, an opening line, the county and why it fits.

What it never does

Contact anyone, make up a fact, list an article twice or pick a big company or bank.

The agent finds. We decide.

Run it every day, and how

Every day, in five steps

  1. Start

    It starts by itself just after 9am, or we type /leads.

  2. Read

    It downloads the eleven feeds.

  3. Skip

    It drops any article it has read before.

  4. Judge

    It keeps only companies that fit our customer.

  5. List

    It adds leads and rebuilds our page.

Each day we open leads.html and say yes or no to each lead.

Scheduled daily from 25 November, while Claude is open on our computer. If it is closed, the run starts when it next opens.
