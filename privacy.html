The short version

Privacy & cookies

We use what you email us to reply and talk about your website, and we don't sell it. We set no cookies, but Vercel and Google Fonts receive your IP address to load these pages.

Who we are

New Path Digital is a small Irish web design team. We're responsible for the information you share with us through this website. You can contact us at hello@newpathdigital.ie.

What we collect, and why

This website has no forms that store your details. If you email us, we receive what you choose to send: usually your name, business, phone or email and the best time to call. We use it to reply to you and to talk about a website for your business. We don't sell it, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

How long we keep it

We keep your emails for as long as we're talking with you about a website, and for as long as you're a client. If you don't become a client, we delete your details within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies. There are no analytics, tracking or advertising tools on it, so there's no cookie banner to click.

Who else gets technical information

Our website is hosted by Vercel, which handles basic technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages to you securely. Our pages also use fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to load them, which shares your IP address with Google. Both companies may handle this information outside Ireland, under their own privacy policies.

Your rights over your information

Under data protection law (GDPR) you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or ask us to stop using it. Just email hello@newpathdigital.ie. If you're not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
