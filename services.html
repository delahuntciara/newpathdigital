Questions about price, changes and leaving

Who owns the website and the domain name?

You do. Your domain name is registered in your name, and your words, photos and logo are yours from day one. After 12 months, the full website is yours to take with you.

What happens if I want to leave?

After the 12-month minimum you can leave any time, and we hand over the full site for free. If you leave before 12 months, you pay for the months that are left.

Is there a big bill up front?

No. €100 a month covers the build.

What counts as a small change?

Prices, opening hours, photos, text, a new service or a new team member. Bigger work, like an online shop or a booking system, is quoted first.

How fast are changes made?

Within 2 working days. Just send us an email or a text.

What is in the monthly marketing insights report?

A short, plain report: how many people visited, how many got in touch, what they searched on Google to find you, and one simple thing to try next month.

What if my Google visits don't go up?

We measure your visits before launch. If your visits or calls haven't gone up after 6 months, we do extra search work for free for the next 3 months.

I already have a website. Can you take it over?

Yes. We build you a new one and move across your domain name and the content you want to keep.

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. Tell us what you want, and we'll design it, build it and look after it.
